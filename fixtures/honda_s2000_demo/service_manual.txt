# Synthetic Honda S2000 Service Manual (demo fixture — not OEM text)

## 1 Engine Oil

### 1-1 Specification
Engine oil capacity with filter change is 4.8 liters (5.1 US quarts).
Use API SJ or later, SAE 10W-30 for normal operating temperatures.
Oil drain plug torque is 39 N·m (29 lbf·ft).

### 1-2 Oil Filter Replacement
1. Warm the engine to normal operating temperature.
2. Remove the oil filler cap.
3. Place a drain pan under the oil drain plug.
4. Remove the drain plug and washer; allow oil to drain completely.
5. Reinstall the drain plug with a new washer; torque to 39 N·m (29 lbf·ft).
6. Remove the oil filter using a filter wrench.
7. Install a new oil filter; hand-tighten then turn an additional 3/4 turn.
8. Refill with 4.8 liters of specified oil; run engine and check for leaks.

## 2 Clutch

### 2-1 Pedal Free Play
Clutch pedal free play should be 9–17 mm (0.35–0.67 in).
If free play is out of specification, adjust the clutch master cylinder pushrod.

### 2-2 Hydraulic Fluid
Use only Honda DOT 3 or DOT 4 brake fluid in the clutch hydraulic system.
Never mix fluid types. Bleed the system after any hydraulic component replacement.

## 3 Spark Plugs

### 3-1 Specification
Spark plug type: NGK PFR7G-11 or equivalent.
Electrode gap: 1.0–1.1 mm (0.039–0.043 in).
Spark plug torque: 18 N·m (13 lbf·ft).

### 3-2 Replacement Interval
Replace spark plugs every 105,000 miles (168,000 km) under normal conditions.
Inspect earlier if misfire DTCs are present.

## 4 Cooling System

### 4-1 Coolant
Coolant capacity is approximately 5.8 liters including reservoir.
Use Honda Type 2 coolant mixed 50/50 with distilled water.
Radiator cap relief pressure is 93–123 kPa (0.95–1.25 kgf/cm², 13.5–17.8 psi).

### 4-2 Thermostat
Thermostat begins opening at 78–82 °C (172–180 °F).
Fully open temperature is 95 °C (203 °F).

## 5 Valve Clearance

### 5-1 Inspection
Valve clearance (cold): intake 0.15–0.19 mm; exhaust 0.26–0.30 mm.
Adjust with the engine cold. Rotate crankshaft to TDC for each cylinder as specified.

## 6 Transmission Fluid

### 6-1 Specification
Manual transmission fluid capacity: 1.5 liters (1.6 US quarts).
Use Honda Manual Transmission Fluid (MTF).
Fill to the bottom of the filler hole with the vehicle level.
